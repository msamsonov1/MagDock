Physics of Electro-Permanent Magnetic (EPM) Couplers in Lunar Vacuum

The MagDock Sentinel resolves a critical vulnerability in lunar autonomous docking: mechanical resistance during insertion can feel fully seated even when ultra-fine, abrasive regolith ($< 50\,\mu\text{m}$) wedges in the pole interface. Because magnetic holding force scales rigorously with the square of flux density ($B^2$), microscopic dust gaps suppress magnetic hold while mechanical actuators stall against false resistance.

Governing Equation 01 Maxwell Stress Tensor

Magnetic Holding Force & Grit Air-Gap Law

The axial retention force $F_{\text{mag}}$ between the rover head and base receptacle is calculated by integrating the Maxwell stress tensor across pole face area $A$. A minute dust layer of thickness $\delta$ and vacuum permeability $\mu_0$ introduces immediate magnetic reluctance:

$$F_{\text{mag}}(\delta) = \frac{B^2(\delta) \cdot A}{2 \mu_0} = \frac{A}{2 \mu_0} \left[ \frac{B_r}{1 + \frac{2 \delta \cdot \mu_r}{L_m}} \right]^2$$

Where $B_r$ is remanence flux density ($\approx 1.45\text{ T}$ for sintered NdFeB/Alnico hybrid cores), $L_m$ is magnetic circuit path length, and $\delta$ is the regolith particle diameter. Notice that a gap of just $\delta = 100\,\mu\text{m}$ degrades clamping force by over $65\%$, leaving the rover susceptible to power disconnection under thermal cable writhing.

Critical Constraint: $F_{\text{mag}} \ge 1200\text{ N}$ Flux Saturation: $B_{\text{sat}} \ge 1.38\text{ T}$
Governing Equation 02 Contact Tribology

Mechanical Granular Jamming & Wedge Force

Lunar regolith features sharp, un-weathered glass shards. When clamped mechanically, inter-grain friction creates force plateau $F_{\text{mech}}$ before true metallic magnetic contact is made:

$$F_{\text{mech}}(z) = k_s (z_{\text{cmd}} - z) + \sum_{i=1}^N \mu_g \cdot \sigma_y \cdot \pi r_i^2 \cdot \Theta(z - z_i)$$

Where $k_s$ is actuator compliance, $\sigma_y$ is regolith compressive yield strength under lunar vacuum, and $\Theta$ is the Heaviside contact step. Linear load cells measure $F_{\text{mech}} \to 1200\text{ N}$, deceiving legacy rovers into declaring successful coupling despite severe gap $\delta > 0$.

Governing Equation 03 Diagnostic Inversion

Phase-Space Inversion & Anomaly Metric ($\Psi$)

The MagDock Sentinel software continuously computes the dynamic correlation metric $\Psi$ in real time between measured force $F(t)$ and differential flux $\Delta B(t)$:

$$\Psi(t) = \frac{B(t)}{\sqrt{\frac{2 \mu_0}{A} F_{\text{mech}}(t)}} \quad \begin{cases} \Psi \ge 0.92 & \implies \text{PASS: True Magnetic Latch} \\ \Psi < 0.92 & \implies \text{ABORT: Grit Entrapment Detected} \end{cases}$$

If $\Psi < 0.92$ when $F \ge 800\text{ N}$, the system triggers an emergency interlock cutoff, disallowing high-voltage microgrid energization and commanding the ultrasonic piezoelectric clearing subroutine.

Governing Equation 04 Acoustic Fluidization

Piezoelectric Micro-Jitter Acoustic Fluidization
